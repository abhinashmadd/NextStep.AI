NextStep Refund & Fee Policy

We believe quality career guidance should be accessible to all students. NextStep's core platform is 100% free with zero hidden fees. For any future optional institutional services, we uphold an unconditional 14-day 100% money-back guarantee.

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 Last Updated: October 11, 2026 Student Cost: $0.00 / ₹0.00 (Free Core Platform)

1. The Zero-Fee Student Promise

NextStep does not believe in charging students hidden fees or trapping users in sneaky recurring subscriptions:

  • No Registration Fees: Creating a student account and setting up your workspace is completely free.
  • No Feature Paywalls: Skill analysis, evidence parsing, RAG query assistance, career frameworks, and next-action recommendations are available with zero payment required.
  • No Hidden Surcharges: There are no processing fees, maintenance fees, data storage charges, or termination penalties.

2. Optional Premium & Institutional Services: 14-Day 100% Refund Guarantee

In the event that you or your academic department purchases an optional institutional verification package, specialized 1-on-1 industry mentor session, or premium cohort license:

14-Day Unconditional Money-Back Guarantee: If you are unsatisfied with any paid service for any reason, you are entitled to a full 100% refund within 14 calendar days of purchase. No questions asked, no cancellation penalties, and no processing deductions.

3. How to Request a Refund

Initiating a refund is straightforward and completely free of friction:

  1. Send an email to refunds@nextstep.edu or billing@nextstep.edu from your registered account email.
  2. Include your Account Username or Order Reference ID.
  3. Our billing team will process your refund confirmation within 24 business hours.
  4. Funds will be credited back to your original payment method (bank account, credit/debit card, or UPI) within 5 to 7 business days depending on your financial institution.

4. Zero Dark Patterns in Billing

Consumer protection guidelines strictly prohibit misleading billing designs:

  • No Automatic Unwanted Rollovers: We never quietly convert free trials into surprise paid recurring billings.
  • No Pre-Selected Checkboxes: Any optional purchases require affirmative, explicit action.
  • One-Click Cancellation: You can downgrade or cancel any subscription at any time with a single click in your account settings.
