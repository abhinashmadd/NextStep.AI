NextStep Cookie Policy

We believe in honest, minimal web technologies. NextStep uses only strictly necessary cookies to authenticate student sessions and safeguard evidence. We do not use advertising or surveillance cookies.

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 Third-Party Ad Trackers: 0 (None) Manage: Open Cookie Settings

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and local storage are small text snippets stored by your browser when you visit a website. They allow the platform to keep you signed in securely, remember your user interface preferences, and protect your workspace against unauthorized tampering.

2. Inventory of Cookies Used on NextStep

Cookie Name Category Lifespan Purpose & Description
nextstep_session Strictly Necessary 30 Days Stores encrypted cryptographic token to verify your authenticated student session. Set with HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax flags to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS).
nextstep_cookie_pref Strictly Necessary 180 Days Remembers your consent decisions regarding optional cookies.
nextstep_token (LocalStorage) Strictly Necessary Persistent (Until Sign Out) Used by client JavaScript for Bearer authorization in API requests.
nextstep_user (LocalStorage) Strictly Necessary Persistent (Until Sign Out) Caches safe public student profile attributes (Name, Target Role, Student ID) to prevent unnecessary redundant network calls.

3. Categories of Cookies

A. Strictly Necessary & Security Cookies (Mandatory)

These cookies are essential for the operation of the NextStep service. Without these cookies, you cannot log in, access your private student dashboard, or upload project evidence. Under ePrivacy Directive and GDPR Article 5(3), these cookies do not require prior consent because they are strictly necessary to deliver the requested service.

B. Performance and Analytics Cookies (Opt-In Only)

These cookies allow us to collect aggregated, non-identifying telemetry regarding API response times and page load speeds. They help us ensure the platform remains fast and responsive. They are disabled by default and require your affirmative consent.

C. Personalization & Preference Cookies (Opt-In Only)

These cookies remember your selected career role filters and visual preferences (such as collapsed sidebar or high-contrast settings). They are disabled by default and require your affirmative consent.

D. Advertising & Tracking Cookies (None)

Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers: NextStep does not partner with advertising networks, behavioral retargeters, or social media tracking pixels (such as Google Ads, Meta Pixel, or TikTok). We will never monetize your browsing habits.

4. Managing and Revoking Your Cookie Preferences

You retain full control over cookies at all times:

  • On-Site Settings Manager: Click Cookie Settings at any time to open the preference manager and modify or revoke your consent.
  • Browser Controls: You can configure your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) to block or delete cookies. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in to your student account.
