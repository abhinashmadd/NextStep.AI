NextStep Privacy Policy

NextStep is built on a simple foundation: your work stays yours. We enforce strict data minimization, store zero extraneous personal information, do not sell student data, and adhere strictly to global privacy frameworks.

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 Last Updated: October 11, 2026 Compliance: GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA (US), DPDP Act (India)

1. Overview and Data Controller

NextStep Career Technologies Inc. (“NextStep”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the NextStep student career-readiness platform. We act as the Data Controller under GDPR/UK GDPR and a Data Fiduciary under the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act).

2. Data Minimization: What We Collect (And What We Don't)

In strict adherence to the Principle of Data Minimization (GDPR Article 5(1)(c)), we only collect information strictly required to provide personalized career skill guidance and evidence assessment:

  • Account Authentication Details: Full name, username, email address, mobile number (used strictly for two-factor/OTP verification and account access), and password (salted using bcrypt with 12 rounds; never stored in plaintext).
  • Academic Context: Degree/course, discipline, academic year, and optional semester.
  • Career & Learning Goals: Target role, preferred learning style, and available weekly study hours.
  • User-Submitted Evidence: Project titles, descriptions, repository links, and user-uploaded portfolio files (e.g., code files, technical write-ups, PDF reports).
  • Self-Assessment & Activity Log: Self-rated skill confidence levels and chronological workspace activity logs.
What We Never Collect: NextStep does not collect financial payment card numbers, social security / national ID numbers, biometric data, precise GPS location, web browsing histories outside of NextStep, or discriminatory sensitive personal attributes.

3. Age Consent and Minors Policy (16+)

NextStep is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 16 years of age. If you are under 16, you may only use this platform with affirmative and verified parental or legal guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 under COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or children under 16 under GDPR-K. If we discover that personal data of an unauthorized minor has been collected, it is immediately purged from our active databases.

5. AI & Knowledge Retrieval Processing (Zero AI Model Training)

NextStep utilizes local deterministic skill matching alongside an integrated NVIDIA NIM RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) inference pipeline:

  • Zero Training on Your Work: Your uploaded documents, project evidence, code, and profile information are never used to train, retrain, or fine-tune artificial intelligence models or public LLMs.
  • Local Processing Priority: Skill taxonomy evaluation and matching occur directly within isolated execution containers.
  • Grounded Citations: AI recommendations cite our open-source, deterministic competency catalog, avoiding speculative hallucinations.

7. Cookie and Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies to maintain secure sessions (nextstep_session) and prevent cross-site request forgery. We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or third-party trackers. For complete transparency, read our Cookie Policy or manage your settings using the Cookie Settings Manager.

8. Your Rights: Access, Export, and Complete Erasure

Regardless of your geographic location, NextStep extends comprehensive privacy rights to every student:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can download a complete JSON export of your workspace at any time via the Privacy Center or by visiting /api/privacy/export.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit your profile details, target roles, or uploaded evidence at any time directly in the workspace.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to Be Forgotten”): You can permanently delete your account, evidence items, and attached files immediately in the Privacy Center, or submit an official request via our Data Deletion Request Portal.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke consent at any time without penalty.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Communications: All notifications include an immediate 1-click unsubscribe option via our Email Preferences Page.

9. Data Retention and Security

Your data is retained only for as long as your account remains active. If you delete an evidence document or delete your account, files are purged immediately from active disk storage and database collections. We employ HTTPS encryption in transit, strict Content Security Policy headers, and rate limiting against brute-force attacks.
