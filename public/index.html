PRIVACY BEFORE PROFILE

Your work stays yours.

Before we collect your profile or evidence, here's exactly what this platform does.

What we collect

Only student profile details, self-assessments, evidence descriptions, and files you explicitly choose to add. We do not collect payment cards, tracking identifiers, or unnecessary personal surveillance data.

How it's processed

This system uses local, deterministic keyword and skill graph analysis with NVIDIA NIM RAG retrieval. Your uploaded work is never used to train public AI models.

Where it's stored & retention

In secure database collections and storage with AES-256 and TLS 1.3 encryption. You retain 100% ownership and can permanently erase your data anytime via the Privacy Center or Deletion Portal.

Age Eligibility & Legal Policies

You must be at least 16 years of age (or have verified parental/guardian consent) to create a portfolio. Review our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Refund Policy.
