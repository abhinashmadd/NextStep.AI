PRIVACY BEFORE PROFILE

Your work stays yours.

Before we collect your profile or evidence, here's exactly what this prototype does.

What we collect

Only the student profile details, self-assessment, evidence descriptions, and files you choose to add.

How it's processed

This prototype uses local, deterministic keyword matching. No external AI service receives your data, and it is not used to train models.

Where it's stored

In a local JSON file and uploads folder on the computer running the app. There is no account system or multi-user access control.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on this device until you export it or use the Privacy Center to delete it. Do not upload sensitive or confidential work.
